# Copywriting Skill

## Goal

Write emotionally engaging landing page copy focused on conversion.

---

# Writing Style

* Clear and concise.
* Emotionally engaging.
* Premium tone.
* Modern and natural language.
* Avoid generic marketing phrases.

---

# CTA Rules

* CTAs should feel direct and inviting.
* Avoid robotic wording.
* Focus on action and experience.

---

# Hero Sections

* Headlines should create emotional impact quickly.
* Avoid long paragraphs.
* Focus on atmosphere and benefits.

---

# Brand Voice

The tone should feel:

* modern
* premium
* social
* energetic
* elegant

Avoid:

* corporate language
* exaggerated claims
* cliché startup phrases
